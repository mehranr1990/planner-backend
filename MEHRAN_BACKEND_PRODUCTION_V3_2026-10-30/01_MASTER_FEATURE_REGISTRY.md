# Master Feature Registry — Mehran Backend

Legend: **LOCAL DONE** = Phase 0/1 backend completion reported and must be independently verified in repo; **APPROVED PRODUCT / NOT BUILT** = owner chose feature but schema/API remains pending; **DEFERRED** = later extension; **OPEN TECH** = decisions needed in approved ADR. All future features below are product-approved unless marked DEFERRED. This is not a claim the API currently supports them.

## Phase 2 — APPROVED PRODUCT / NOT BUILT
Shared custody/security/membership kernel as first prerequisite. Independent Projects or Team/Company-owned projects. Sections/Phases/Milestones, **no nested subprojects**. Owner/Admin/Manager/Member/Viewer with capability matrix. Verified-user invitations, external verified-account collaborators limited by explicit grants (not anonymous/unregistered guests). One primary assignee + contributors, assignment Pending/Accepted/Declined, reason on decline, organization-mandated assignment only under explicit policy. Accepted external tasks visible as authorized projection in My Tasks/Calendar; Time Block creation configurable, NOT a copy/transfer. Versioned custom status/workflow, controlled transitions and approvals. Task dependencies FS/SS/FF/SF, constrained lag/lead, cycle prevention, reschedule only with confirmation. Typed custom fields: text/number/date/boolean/single/multi-select/person/currency. Estimates, work time, workload/capacity and warnings without personal focus leakage. Project templates; lists, Kanban, calendar, timeline, Gantt, dashboard (view rendering belongs to frontend). Risks/issues with severity/probability/owner/mitigation/history. Bulk operations, bounded CSV import. Authorized project file, comments, activity, reports. Controlled conversion from personal to collaborative/project transfer with privacy preview, approval. Archive/restore/retention-aware deletion. **OPEN TECH:** schema, transaction fencing, custody transfer scope, exact API/workflow definitions.

## Phase 3 — APPROVED PRODUCT / NOT BUILT
Independent Teams or Company-related Teams; multiple memberships across companies. Team Owner/Admin/Lead/Member/Viewer, per-Project grants (team membership alone does NOT expose every project). Workload from explicitly work-approved inputs; weekly planning, optional sprints, backlog/reviews. Team dashboards and **portfolio** across multiple permitted projects. Base meeting event/invite/agenda (no chat engine yet). Transparent non-covert work performance metrics. Recognition (private/public as authorized), feedback, documented corrective actions with response/appeal, default confidential, bounded authorized disclosure; no automatic monetary punishment. Team transition/attach-to-company with reviewed custody and membership changes, archive/restore/deletion.

## Phase 4 — APPROVED PRODUCT / NOT BUILT
Multiple independent Company workspaces; Company -> Department/Unit -> Team with multi-membership. Owner/Org Admin/Department Manager/Team Lead/Member/Viewer and explicit exception-safe policy inheritance. One company custody boundary unless approved reason for more; departments/projects are not automatically independent data Spaces. Lifecycle, transfer of workspace administrative ownership (NOT legal title), last-custodian protection. Work directory/profile and **separate sensitive employee file** with document-level authorization/retention/audit. Capacity, staff and equipment allocation; basic working availability only, HR/Payroll advanced DEFERRED. Recognition/corrective actions as Phase 3 with corporate policies. Company Expense, Budget and Allocation at Company/Department/Project level; claim/review/approval, receipt, commitment, employee Reimbursement and recorded settlement. Distinguish payment claim from confirmed payment; no full double-entry accounting, tax or payroll. Versioned approval workflow for expenses/resources/requests. Company KPI/project/portfolio/expense/overrun reports, private knowledge base folders/versions/search, authorized export/audit. CRM, detailed HR, contract systems, ticketing, full accounting = DEFERRED extensions.

## Phase 5 — APPROVED PRODUCT / NOT BUILT
One durable conversation/message engine: independent DMs, private multi-person conversations, Project/Team/Company channels, prepared for Social Group context. Rich-but-bounded text, pictures, attachments/links and short recorded voice messages (max **2 minutes AND 10 MB**, both enforced), no built-in live audio/video calling, no v1 E2EE. Reply, thread, reaction, edit, delete, forward with safe grant, pin, mention, search. Persistent PostgreSQL source and ordered per-conversation history; authenticated REST history and frontend-driven cursor-based incremental polling, durable outbox and bounded replay/catch-up under owner-approved [ADR 0044](../docs/adr/0044-phase-5-rest-polling-and-realtime-deferral.md). P5-04 WebSocket/presence/live typing/connection management is DEFERRED; Push Notifications remain P5-03 scope; sent/delivered/read with scoped policies. DM Block prevents direct contact; shared-group participation/messages remain possible and separately governable, block suppresses unwanted direct contact/presence/mentions. Moderation/report, Mute, mention-only quiet hours; membership revoked on every read/send/subscription. Messages: **edit up to 48h**, normal author delete-for-everyone within 48h in DM/private groups; workplace-channel delete-for-everyone within 48h only when authorized policy allows; after 48h normal user deletion is **delete-for-self** and other participants still see message; administrative removal, safety/legal retention separate. DM delete and workspace retention policies source/context-specific, versioned, server-enforced; newcomer private-group history only from join by default, work-channel history governed by authorized policy. Account deletion removes/pseudonymizes identity where required while preserving authorized conversation coherence. Meeting personal/project/team/company: agenda, RSVP, minutes, decisions, action items linked to authoritative Task, draft/review/approve/final, attachments, external meeting URL. Announcements with targeting, pin, optional read acknowledgement. **OPEN TECH:** exact retention durations, compatible media MIME, WebSocket topology/protocol, read receipt semantics and moderation policy.

## Phase 6 — APPROVED PRODUCT / NOT BUILT
Connections via mutual acceptance, verified email/optional verified phone discovery with anti-enumeration/rate limits and privacy controls. Circles = **private contact labels** not membership boundaries. Social Groups = distinct social domain with shared-custody/membership security, Owner/Admin/Member, private by default, discoverable groups opt-in with approval REQUIRED to join. Activity independent or under group; generic templates for travel/sport/family/gatherings, multi-day itineraries, RSVPs Going/Maybe/Declined/Pending, caps/waitlist, consent-based free/busy, polls and scheduling, checklists, tasks, agenda, files, reminders and group chat via Phase 5. Social expenses at Group/Activity level: all verified, authorized expense participants in a Group or independent Activity may SUBMIT, but only APPROVED records affect split balances. Equal/percent/fixed/weighted/selected participants; exact minor-unit allocation and currency; approved edit creates pending revision while current approved version remains effective. Each participant sees their own share/paid/owed/receivable, controlled aggregate visibility. Settlement recorded by payer and confirmed by counterparty; does NOT transfer bank funds. Optional explicit idempotent opt-in transfer of one's amount to Personal Finance without double posting. Lending: personal or group assets, owner may claim 'item is with X' but borrower must confirm; request/accept/handover/return, overdue/dispute/lost/damaged with evidence; **Lost & Found** posts and claim ('mine is missing'/'I have found it') with verified resolution. Albums: PRIVATE, image max **20MB per original**, thumbnails/optimized display, total workspace quota configurable later via Admin; bounded processing/deletion. Voluntary social group goals/challenges; no auto-publication of private habits. Departures preserve appropriate shared transaction history, settle responsibility and revoke access.

## Consistent global features/constraints
Single Better Auth user across all contexts; verified accounts required for membership, edits, RSVP and expense submissions; invitation preview may show safe metadata prior to verified signup. Cross-space permission-filtered My Tasks, Goals, Calendar overlays, search, notifications, activity feed. Persian/English, RTL/LTR, Jalali/Gregorian, Sat-start localization, Persian digits, Toman/Rial display via frontend and exact server money/time contracts. Modular feature enablement without conflating hiding UI and authorization. Full data isolation; no Journal/Mood/personal finance visibility through shared access. Native voice/video call absent; audio file messages approved. AI general assistant, automation execution, third-party API integrations, advanced HR/ERP, full accounting and actual payment movement deferred. No cross-space generic automatic sync beyond P1-12.

## Cross-chat nuances and explicit status notes
- Authentication/identity already includes a two-step verified email-change process in the reported P0 baseline; do not omit this from the audit. Future phone verification is **not** an existing Phase 1 feature.
- Personal Planner historical requirements additionally include energy/context tags, Quick Capture triage, free-slot suggestions, personal finance Riyal/Toman representation, Persian digit setting, Saturday-first week and bilingual EN/FA contracts; each must be marked **verified implemented / planned UI / deferred / not found** by source audit, rather than inferred from this summary. Natural-language Quick Add and fully automated planning/rollovers should not be claimed implemented solely on the basis of generic Task endpoints.
- A Team/Company-owned Project is an approved eventual topology, but Team/Company domains do not yet exist in Phase 2. Phase 2 must implement independent Projects plus **reviewed forward-compatible association interfaces**; do not create fake future Company/Team records or claim live Company attachment until the owning domain is delivered.
- Verified external collaborator != unregistered guest: any write, RSVP, group admission, shared expense submission or chat participation requires a verified account and explicit narrow authority. Anonymous invite preview may expose only expressly public safe metadata.
- Independent Activity expense participants must be eligible to submit a proposal without first creating or joining a permanent Social Group, subject to verified account and Activity permission.
- Chat in Phase 5 must work for unrelated people without Team/Company membership. The user's choice to add **recorded voice messages** is a deliberate exception to the earlier no-native-live-audio/video-call decision.
- Phase 7 Admin should allow dynamic price/plan/quota assignment *without rewriting owning domains*; pre-Phase-7 slices must use stable machine-readable feature keys, usage owner/count semantics and decision interfaces—not mere comments or placeholders. Future pricing still requires owner approval and an independent paid-launch gate.
- Social `Lost & Found` is broader than formal lending: allow a report of a missing item and a counterpart's voluntary `I found/have it` claim; ownership, custody and resolution remain unverified until agreed.
