## Sources and decisions applied

These sources were read:

- Frontend: `design/team/{DECISIONS,SCREENS,SPEC}.md`, `src/data/team-repositories.ts`, `src/data/team-domain.ts`, and the Teams rows of `docs/integration/API_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md` and `BACKEND_REQUIREMENTS.md`.
- Backend: the P3-02 and P3-03 contracts, ADR 0030 and ADR 0033.

The owner decisions from 2026-11-13 were applied as follows:

- **Presence** is manual only, with four values: `AVAILABLE`, `BUSY`, `AWAY`, `OFFLINE`. The server never infers a status.
- **Check-ins** have a manager-configured `DAILY` or `WEEKLY` cadence. There is no default schedule.
- **Feedback** is visible only to the parties involved. Owner, Admin and Lead roles do not grant access to it.
- **Recognition** is visible to authorized Team members only. Private thanks are visible only to the writer and the recipients.
- **Privacy by default:**
  - `statusEnabled` and `checkinsEnabled` are stored as `null` until a manager sets them. The features stay off while the value is `null`.
  - No notification, schedule or retention values were invented.
  - ADR 0033 confidential cases and disclosures are unchanged. The new thanks and feedback records are separate, lightweight records under the owner direction of 2026-11-13.

## New API: 23 operations, 864 → 887

All paths are under `/api/v1/teams/{id}`. Every operation uses these existing controls:

- a verified session, `TeamGuard`, strict Zod validation and the trusted Origin check on mutations;
- `no-store` responses;
- current Team admission and generation, through the Shared kernel;
- audit and outbox entries through `offerEffect`.

`If-Match` is required where marked. `Idempotency-Key` is required on every create.

Changes to existing operations (response shapes are unchanged):

- `PATCH /teams/{id}/plans/{planId}` now records publication and close metadata.
- Activating a second sprint now returns 409 `ACTIVE_SPRINT` (design rule).

## Migration `202611130048_team_completion` (strictly additive; not applied to any persistent database)

- **New tables:** `TeamSetting`, `TeamPresence`, `TeamCheckinReply`, `TeamCheckinComment`, `TeamTaskState`, `TeamThanks`, `TeamThanksRecipient`, `TeamFeedbackRequest`, `TeamFeedbackNote`. Each has Team, member, admission-generation and actor foreign keys, plus shape checks.
- **New nullable or defaulted columns:** on `TeamPlan`, `focus`, `focusByActorId`, `focusAt`, `goal`, `publishedAt`, `publishedByActorId`, `closedAt`; on `TeamWorkItem`, `carriedFromPlanId` and `plannedAt`.
- **What the migration avoids:** no updates, deletes, triggers or functions.
- **Lifecycle integration:** all nine tables are registered in `TeamCustodyGraph` (Company custody moves) and `TeamCleanupService` (children are deleted first).
- **Gate:** `pnpm db:migrations:check` passes. The table count it expects changed from 245 to 254.

## Owner decisions still required

1. **Approve the FK-catalog re-pin and the transfer-catalog entry.** This blocks all Shared lifecycle cleanup once the migration is applied.
2. **Team task assignments.** This means extending the Project-owned `TaskAssignment` consent engine to Team custody, which P3-02 excluded. It also needs the ADR 0030 `/me/work` Personal projections, which are not implemented. The following depend on it:
   - assignees,
   - `forYou`,
   - accept, decline, revert and remind,
   - removal preview,
   - "now working on",
   - Members planning their own tasks.
3. **Capacity model.** Choose between the T6 weekly hours plus working days with estimate-based booked %, and the approved P3-03 per-date capacity with assignment-based workload.
4. **Daily check-in late window.** None was applied. Weekly uses the 24h window from T12.
5. **Check-in reminder** delivery channel and timing.
6. **Team file-library model**, including retention and quota.
7. **Goal key results and periods.**
8. **Story-point storage** and the **Weekly-to-Sprints conversion** semantics.
9. **Project health** definition and **Team ↔ Project people correlation**.
10. **Member-authored time-off events.**
11. **"Members may invite"**, Viewer booking visibility, and job title.
12. **Existing items:** OD-1 (`retentionPolicyVersion` for Team create), OD-4 (invitation proof delivery), OD-14 (Shared activation).

What prevents full Live Mode for Teams:

- items 1 and 12;
- the migration has not yet been applied to the target database;
