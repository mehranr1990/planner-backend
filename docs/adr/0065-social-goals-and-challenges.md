# 0065: Social Goals and Challenges (P6-05); Albums deferred

Status: **APPROVED by the owner for decisions P6-05-D1 to D5** (2026-11-18). Product rules come
from approved sources (V3 03 "opt-in social goals/challenges; no automatic private habit
disclosure", ADR 0051 "challenge ranking is off"). The engineering design below was accepted
with this batch. **Photo Albums are DEFERRED** by owner decision; only a future contract exists
([social-albums-deferred-contract](../architecture/social-albums-deferred-contract.md)).
Social retention durations are **not** approved by this record.
Companion contracts: ADR 0044 (polling, no realtime), 0050/0051 (Group roles, organizers),
0057/0063 (files blocked), 0058 (rate limits), 0060 (Activity authority, 30-day recovery).

## D1 — Group and Activity scope

| Item                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Class                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Goals/Challenges exist in Social Groups and in independent or Group-associated Activities, each with exactly one authoritative context; no second custody system.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | APPROVED OWNER POLICY                                 |
| `SocialGoal` has `groupId` XOR `activityId` (DB CHECK `num_nonnulls = 1`) and CASCADEs with its context. Every request derives authority from the existing model: Group members via the Group custody fence (`task.read`), Activity content authority via `ActivityRuntime.access` (accepted participation; Group-derived participants lose it with membership; guests keep only their Activity). | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Organizers are the existing context managers, not a new role: Group Owner/Admin (`settings.manage`); Activity organizer or its Group Owner/Admin (`ActivityAccess.manage`) — the ADR 0051 approver set. Only organizers create, edit, close, cancel, review and correct.                                                                                                                          | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| The Personal `Goal` engine is Space/custody-scoped and cannot own Activity-context collaborative progress, so a Social-specific extension is used. Personal Goals, Habits and other private records are never read, copied, migrated or exposed.                                                                                                                                                  | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |

## D2 — Progress submission and approval

| Item                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Class                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Participants submit their own progress; organizers may review; review is configurable per goal (`reviewRequired`, default `false` = ordinary self-reporting); explicit audited corrections; no external verification service.                                                                                                                     | APPROVED OWNER POLICY                                 |
| `POST /entries` has no participant field: the submitter is always the caller. Entries are `PENDING` when review is required, else `APPROVED` (self-reported, `reviewedAt = null`). Only `APPROVED` entries count. Rejection changes no total. A client entry `id` plus an idempotency receipt prevent double counting.                            | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Review: organizer only; never the submitter (`SELF_REVIEW_FORBIDDEN`); `PENDING` only; `If-Match` entry revision. The submitter may retract a `PENDING` entry or an unreviewed self-report; a reviewed approval cannot be retracted.                                                                                                              | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Corrections: `POST /corrections` creates a signed `CORRECTION` entry (organizer, mandatory reason, optional `correctsEntryId` of the same participant's approved submission, `If-Match` participant revision). Never on oneself; never below zero. The original submission is unchanged.                                                          | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Submitter, reviewer, timestamps, review note, state and revision are stored on each entry; `SocialGoalEvent` keeps the append-only history.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Owner decision (P6-05 acceptance, 2026-11-18): no participant approves their own progress, including a sole organizer. When review is required and no other eligible reviewer exists, the organizer's own submission stays `PENDING`. No auto-approval, no temporary reviewer privilege, no new role. Self-reporting without review is unchanged. | APPROVED OWNER POLICY                                 |
| Enforced by `SELF_REVIEW_FORBIDDEN` on review and on corrections of one's own record. `SocialGoal.viewer.independentReviewerAvailable` is `true` only when a current organizer other than the caller exists, so the client can warn before submitting to a review-required goal.                                                                  | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |

## D3 — Participant progress visibility

| Item                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Class                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Authorized participants see each other's Social progress; only progress recorded in the goal; no leakage outside the context.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | APPROVED OWNER POLICY                                 |
| Context members see the goal and its aggregate. Individual progress, entries and history: organizers see all current context members; active participants see active peers' approved totals and approved entries, plus everything of their own; a withdrawn member sees only their own record. Pending totals and pending/rejected entries are visible to the submitter and organizers only. | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Visibility is evaluated per request. Losing Group membership or Activity participation removes access and hides the person from peers' lists, entries, history and the participant count immediately. Outsiders receive safe 404s; lists are context-scoped.                                                                                                                                 | IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED                              |

## D4 — Voluntary participation

| Item                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Class                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Joining is voluntary and explicit with no organizer approval; no automatic enrollment; withdrawal allowed; revocation of context access revokes goal access.                                                                                                                                                    | APPROVED OWNER POLICY                                 |
| `POST /join` by a current content participant only. No create, invite or announcement path enrolls anyone. `UNIQUE(goalId, userId)` plus the goal row lock prevent duplicate or racing joins. Withdrawal (`If-Match`) retracts the person's pending entries and keeps approved progress; rejoining restores it. | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Joins close when the window ends, the goal is not `ACTIVE`, the Activity is not `PLANNED`, or a collective goal is already complete.                                                                                                                                                                            | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |

## D5 — Progress, completion and badges

| Item                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Class                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Progress, percentage, completion status and a simple completion badge. No leaderboards, rankings, XP, money, penalties, paid incentives, automated Habit publishing or global feeds. Completion is deterministic and auditable.                                                                                                                                                                   | APPROVED OWNER POLICY                                 |
| `CHALLENGE`: each active participant completes when their approved total ≥ target. `GOAL`: completes when the collective approved total ≥ target; participants active at that moment get the badge. Percent = `floor(total × 100 / target)`, capped at 100. A correction or target edit below the target revokes the badge (`badge.revoked`). Withdrawn participants keep their recorded outcome. | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Quantities are whole units of the goal's unit (1 to 10⁹). Lists are ordered by id; no endpoint sorts or ranks people by progress.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |

## Lifecycle, dates, notices and data

| Item                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Class                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| States `ACTIVE` → `CLOSED` or `CANCELED` (organizer, `If-Match`). Closed goals accept no joins or submissions; pending entries remain reviewable and correctable. Canceled goals are read-only; their pending entries never count. Goals are never deleted by users.                                                                                                                                                 | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| `startsOn`/`endsOn` are calendar days in the goal's IANA `timezone`; window `UPCOMING`/`OPEN`/`ENDED` is derived. Submissions need an `OPEN` window and `occurredOn` within the window and not after today in that zone. Window ≤ 731 days; a goal cannot be created already ended.                                                                                                                                  | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Notices reuse the Social inbox with the goal's Group/Activity context column: `GOAL_ENTRY_SUBMITTED` (managers, review-required only), `GOAL_ENTRY_DECIDED`, `GOAL_CORRECTED`, `GOAL_COMPLETED`, `GOAL_ENDED`. Payload is kind + goal id; recipients must hold current context authority. Migration 0082 is a reviewed, hash-pinned kind-CHECK replacement; the enum widening is a reviewed compatibility extension. | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Writes run inside the existing Group or Activity fence, then lock the goal row; every mutation is idempotent (`Idempotency-Key`) and fenced by `If-Match` where state is replaced. `SocialGoalEvent` + the transactional outbox record each change. Rate policy: `social-goals` is in the Social family; close/cancel, review and correction actions are sensitive.                                                  | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Erasure: active participation ends, pending entries are retracted, identity is detached (SET NULL) and the person's notes and review notes are removed. Approved amounts stay as anonymous contributions so others' collective totals and badges reconcile. Export includes the person's participations and entries.                                                                                                 | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN, IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED |
| Activity-context goals are Activity content: disposed with the Activity after its 30-day recovery window (ADR 0060 B). Group-context goals follow the Group purge cascade.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | ACCEPTED ENGINEERING DESIGN                           |
| Retention durations for goal history and anonymous contributions; final disposal outside the cascades above.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | POLICY-GATED (P6-06)                                  |
