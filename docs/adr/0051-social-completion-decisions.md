# 0051: Social completion — confirmed owner policies

Status: **APPROVED POLICY**, owner replies in this session, 2026-11-13.
Execution: owner authorized sequential Social completion; no Shared activation,
live tests, destructive migrations, commits, pushes or deployment.

The owner explicitly approved all seven S3–S9 proposals from the Social design:

- FIFO waitlist; offer a released place for 12 elapsed hours, not automatic attendance.
- Expense approvers: Owner/Admin and the Activity organizer in the Activity scope.
- Members see their own balances; administrators see all balances in their authorized scope.
- Exact split remainder: payer first, then the recorded participant order.
- Settlement “not received” disputes carry a note; settlement records never move money.
- Album display copies omit location metadata.
- Challenge ranking is off; optional individual totals do not introduce ranks.

The owner separately approved explicit acceptance before Activity/chat content,
current-chat-join history, and Activity guests limited to their invited Activity.
Guest admission grants no Group overview, roster, inventory, album or other
Activity authority. Apply this restriction over the contradictory guest inventory
permissions in SO-10. Group expense authority is distinct from participation in an
Activity's own expenses. Group membership does not silently enroll chat participants.

Unchanged: ADR 0050 admission/role ceilings, verified-account requirements, private
Circles, discovery consent and pair-block behavior; ADRs 0045–0049 message privacy,
author windows, explicit participation and independent erasure. No debt transfer
(c09), new legal retention duration, always-on free/busy audience, image codec or
new dependency is approved by these replies. Retention provisioning remains an
operator responsibility; image-processing implementation approval is still required
by V3 document `03_PHASE_2_TO_7_BACKEND_SPEC.md` before an album pipeline can be
claimed complete.

Implementation evidence and any additional decisions belong in the sequential
completion reports. Product approval is not production or migration-application
acceptance.
